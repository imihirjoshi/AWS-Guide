Amazon Managed Streaming for Apache Kafka

Analytics  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

AWS runs Apache Kafka for you so you do not have to babysit the servers.

Picture it as

Hiring a managed office building instead of buying one. Same offices, someone else fixes the boiler.

What it actually does

Kafka is the industry standard event log. MSK runs the brokers, patching and monitoring, in two forms, provisioned, where you pick broker sizes, and Serverless, which scales itself. It is real Kafka, so your existing clients, Connect plugins and tools work unchanged. Note that AWS manages the infrastructure, not your topics, partitions or consumer lag, which are still your job and still the usual source of pain.

Reach for it when

  • You already use Kafka and want to stop running brokers
  • You need Kafka-only features such as Connect, compaction or exactly-once semantics
  • Moving an on-premises Kafka estate to AWS

Do not reach for it when

  • Starting fresh on AWS with no Kafka history, Kinesis Data Streams is simpler and cheaper to run
  • For a simple task queue, use Amazon SQS
  • For fan-out notifications, use Amazon SNS or EventBridge

One small example

A retailer lifts its on-premises Kafka cluster into AWS and its dozens of existing producers keep working untouched.

How you pay

Per broker hour plus storage, billed continuously, and a small cluster across three zones costs real money every month even when idle. Cross-zone data transfer is the line people do not expect.
