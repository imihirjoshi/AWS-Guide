Amazon Detective

Security and Identity  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

After an alert, it shows you the story of what actually happened.

Picture it as

CCTV footage you scrub back through after the alarm goes off, to see who came in and where they went.

What it actually does

Detective pulls CloudTrail, VPC Flow Logs and GuardDuty findings into a linked graph, so you can follow a suspicious role or IP across time and accounts without writing queries. It is for investigating, not detecting: it finds nothing on its own and is only useful once GuardDuty or Security Hub has raised something. Teams that never investigate alerts get no value from it.

Reach for it when

  • You get GuardDuty findings and have to work out the blast radius
  • Tracing what one compromised role touched across accounts
  • You do not want to hand-write Athena queries over CloudTrail

Do not reach for it when

  • Useless without GuardDuty, turn that on first
  • If nobody investigates alerts, this is money for a screen nobody opens
  • For ad hoc log searching across many sources, Security Lake plus Athena is more flexible

One small example

An EC2 instance is flagged for talking to a known bad IP, and Detective shows it also assumed a role and listed every S3 bucket.

How you pay

Per gigabyte of log data ingested per account, per month. Chatty accounts with high VPC flow log volume drive it.
