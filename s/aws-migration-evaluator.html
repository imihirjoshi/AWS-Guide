AWS Migration Evaluator

Migration and Transfer  ·  Common, learn when you need it

Looks at the servers you own today and estimates what they would cost on AWS.

Picture it as

A removal company walking through your house and quoting the van size before you pack.

What it actually does

You run a small collector program in your own data centre for a few weeks. It measures how busy each server really is, then AWS produces a report matching each one to an EC2 size, with a cost estimate including licences. It is an assessment report, not something that moves any workload, and the useful version comes through an AWS team rather than fully self service. It came from TSO Logic, a company AWS bought in 2019.

Reach for it when

  • Management wants a number before approving a move to AWS
  • You suspect your servers are far bigger than the work needs
  • You need a licence cost comparison for Windows or SQL Server

Do not reach for it when

  • You only have a handful of servers. Price them by hand with aws-cost-explorer style guesses instead.
  • You already decided to move and want the actual migration tooling. This only produces a business case.
  • You want ongoing cost control after you are on AWS. Use aws-cost-explorer and aws-budgets.

One small example

A company finds 300 servers averaging 8 percent CPU and the report shows a far smaller EC2 bill than expected.

How you pay

No charge for the assessment itself. The real cost is the weeks of data collection and the people time.
