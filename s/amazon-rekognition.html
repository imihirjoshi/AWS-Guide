Amazon Rekognition

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Common, learn when you need it

Understands what is in a picture or video.

Picture it as

A person who can glance at a photo and tell you what is in it.

What it actually does

Rekognition detects objects, text, unsafe content and faces in images and video. Face features carry real privacy and legal weight, so check the law before using them on people.

Reach for it when

  • Moderating uploaded images
  • Finding text in photos
  • Counting objects

Do not reach for it when

  • Reading a structured document. Use Textract.

One small example

Automatically rejecting an uploaded profile photo that is not a face.

How you pay

Per image or per minute of video.

Usually sits next to

Amazon TextractPulls text and tables out of scanned documents.Amazon Simple Storage ServiceA place to keep files, as many as you want.
