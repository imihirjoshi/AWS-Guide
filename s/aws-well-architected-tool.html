AWS Well Architected Tool

Management and Governance  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

A questionnaire about your system that produces a list of the risks you have not handled.

Picture it as

A building safety checklist you fill in yourself. The form does not inspect the building, it just makes sure you thought about the fire exits.

What it actually does

You answer questions across six areas such as security, cost and reliability, and it produces a scored report of high and medium risks with links to guidance. Every answer comes from you, so the output is only as honest as your answers. It is mostly used by consultants and by teams doing a formal review before a big launch.

Reach for it when

  • A structured review before a major launch
  • Partner or audit work that needs a documented review
  • Getting a team to agree on where the weak spots are

Do not reach for it when

  • Finding real problems in a live account, use Trusted Advisor, Security Hub or Compute Optimizer which actually look at your resources
  • A small side project, the review takes hours and tells you little

One small example

A team answers the reliability questions, realises nobody has ever tested the database restore, and logs it as a high risk.

How you pay

The tool is free. The cost is the time, and any consultant you pay to run the review.
