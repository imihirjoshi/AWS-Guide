AWS Auto Scaling

Management and Governance  ·  Common, learn when you need it

One screen to set up scaling across several services at once.

Picture it as

A master thermostat for the whole house instead of one dial per room.

What it actually does

It is a front end over EC2 Auto Scaling and Application Auto Scaling. You pick an app, say whether you care more about cost or availability, and it sets scaling policies on the pieces. Most engineers skip it and configure the underlying services directly, often through infrastructure as code. It gets little attention and is easy to mistake for EC2 Auto Scaling, which is the one people actually mean.

Reach for it when

  • A first, quick scaling setup across a small stack
  • Wanting one plan covering EC2 and DynamoDB together

Do not reach for it when

  • Anything defined in Terraform or CloudFormation, set the underlying policies directly
  • A single service, go straight to EC2 Auto Scaling or Application Auto Scaling

One small example

You point it at a web app and it puts target tracking on both the EC2 group and the DynamoDB table.

How you pay

Free. You pay only for the capacity that gets added.
