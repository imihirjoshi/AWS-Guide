AWS AppFabric

Business Applications  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It pulls the activity logs out of your SaaS apps and puts them in one place.

Picture it as

Every shop in a mall keeps its own visitor book in its own format. AppFabric collects all the books nightly and rewrites them in one common format so security can actually read them.

What it actually does

You connect SaaS tools like Slack, Zoom, Okta or Asana, and AppFabric fetches their audit logs, converts them to the OCSF standard schema and drops them in S3 or sends them to a security tool. It is read-only plumbing for logs and user lists, not a way to control those apps. The catch is the connector list: if your app is not on it, AppFabric does nothing for you.

Reach for it when

  • You run many SaaS apps and want their audit logs in one SIEM
  • Security asks who did what across tools
  • You want a user inventory across SaaS apps

Do not reach for it when

  • Your apps are not on the supported connector list, write to the vendor APIs or use your SIEM vendor's own connectors
  • You want to sync data or automate actions between SaaS apps, that is a job for EventBridge or an iPaaS like Workato

One small example

A 300 person company sends Slack, Zoom and Okta audit logs through AppFabric into Splunk so one query can find every login from a new country.

How you pay

Charged per GB of log data ingested, plus the S3 storage and the SIEM ingestion cost on the other side. The SIEM bill is usually the bigger surprise.
