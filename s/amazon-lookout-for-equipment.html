Amazon Lookout for Equipment

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Learns the normal readings of a machine and warns when they drift, and it is being shut down.

Picture it as

A mechanic who knows how your car normally sounds and says something is off before it breaks.

What it actually does

You feed it sensor history from an industrial machine, it learns normal behaviour with no failure labels needed, then flags abnormal periods and says which sensors drove the flag. AWS closed it to new customers and support ends on 7 October 2026, so do not start anything new on it. You need existing sensors and a data historian already in place.

Reach for it when

  • Nothing new. Existing users should be planning a move

Do not reach for it when

  • Any new predictive maintenance project, use SageMaker anomaly detection or a vendor platform like your historian's own tooling
  • Sites with no sensors, you need hardware first

One small example

A pump's vibration and temperature drift together for three days before a bearing fails.

How you pay

Charged for data ingest, training hours and per hour of inference scheduling. Not worth planning around now.
