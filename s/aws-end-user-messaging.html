AWS End User Messaging

Business Applications  ·  Common, learn when you need it

It sends SMS, voice calls and phone push notifications from your code.

Picture it as

A post office counter that will send a text, place a recorded call or ping someone's phone for you, and handles the paperwork with the phone companies.

What it actually does

This is the SMS, MMS, voice and mobile push part of Amazon Pinpoint, renamed in 2024 and kept alive while the rest of Pinpoint is retired. It handles phone numbers, sender IDs, carrier registration and one-time passcodes. It sends messages, it does not decide who to message or track campaigns, that logic is yours. Note the API and SDK clients are still called pinpoint, which confuses people.

Reach for it when

  • Sending one-time passcodes or login codes
  • Order and delivery alerts by SMS
  • Push notifications to your iOS or Android app

Do not reach for it when

  • You want email, use Amazon SES instead
  • You want campaign scheduling, segments and journeys, that was Pinpoint and it ends 30 October 2026, look at Amazon Connect outbound campaigns or a third party like Braze

One small example

A food delivery app texts a six digit login code and later pushes a notification when the rider is two minutes away.

How you pay

Per message, and the price varies a lot by destination country. What surprises people is the per number monthly rental plus one-off carrier registration fees, for example US 10DLC registration.
