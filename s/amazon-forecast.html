Amazon Forecast

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Predicted future numbers from your past numbers, and is closed to new customers.

Picture it as

A shopkeeper's notebook that guesses next month's sales from the last three years, and the notebook is no longer sold.

What it actually does

You uploaded a history of dated values, plus extras like price and holidays, and it trained time series models and returned predictions with a range, not a single number. AWS closed it to new customers on 29 July 2024 and adds no new features. Existing users can still run it. New work goes to Amazon SageMaker Canvas, which does the same forecasting job.

Reach for it when

  • You are an existing customer with pipelines already built

Do not reach for it when

  • Any new forecasting project, use SageMaker Canvas or a normal library like Prophet on a small instance
  • Forecasting a handful of series, a spreadsheet or simple script will do

One small example

A retailer predicted per-store demand per product for the next six weeks to decide reorders.

How you pay

Charged for data storage, training hours and per thousand forecasts. Training runs on hundreds of thousands of series were the expensive part.
