Amazon Monitron

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

A sensor you stick on a motor that warns when it starts running badly, no longer sold to new customers.

Picture it as

A smoke alarm for a machine. You stick it on, it beeps on your phone when something changes.

What it actually does

An end to end kit: a battery powered vibration and temperature sensor, a gateway, a phone app and a service that learns normal and sends alerts. It was the no-expertise option for factories. AWS closed it to new customers on 31 October 2024 and stopped device sales in July 2025, honouring warranties and support for existing users. Do not plan new deployments.

Reach for it when

  • Nothing new. Existing installs continue under warranty

Do not reach for it when

  • New condition monitoring, buy from a vibration sensor vendor with its own platform
  • Anything needing custom sensor types, Monitron only ever read vibration and temperature

One small example

A sensor on a conveyor motor sends a phone alert that vibration has climbed out of its normal band.

How you pay

You bought the sensors and gateways, then paid a yearly per sensor fee. The recurring per sensor fee across hundreds of machines was the part people underestimated.
