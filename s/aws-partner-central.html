AWS Partner Central

Management and Governance  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

The website AWS resellers and software vendors use to manage their AWS partnership.

Picture it as

The staff entrance of a shopping centre, not a shop you buy from.

What it actually does

This is a business portal, not a technical service. Companies in the AWS Partner Network sign in here to list products on AWS Marketplace, register sales deals with AWS, claim partner benefits and get training. It has now absorbed the old AWS Marketplace Management Portal. It runs nothing and stores none of your application data.

Reach for it when

  • Your company sells software or consulting and wants to list it on AWS Marketplace
  • You need to register a customer deal so AWS sales will co-sell with you
  • Tracking your partner tier and required certifications

Do not reach for it when

  • If you are an AWS customer rather than an AWS partner, you will never open this.
  • If you only want to buy software, use AWS Marketplace.
  • If you want technical support on a service, use AWS Support, not this portal.

One small example

A consultancy logs in to register a deal with a retail client so AWS sales does not compete with them on the same account.

How you pay

Free to use. Some partner programmes have their own annual fees or spend requirements, and Marketplace takes a cut of what you sell through it.
