AWS Thinkbox Stoke

Media  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Free 3ds Max plugin that simulates particles moving through fields of motion.

Picture it as

Dropping confetti into a wind tunnel and watching where it goes.

What it actually does

This is desktop software for Autodesk 3ds Max only, not a cloud service. It generates and simulates particles driven by velocity fields, and reads simulation data from FumeFX, Particle Flow, thinkingParticles and RealFlow. It is free now, along with every Thinkbox product, and is in maintenance rather than active development. It is usually used to feed Krakatoa.

Reach for it when

  • Adding drifting debris or spray on top of an existing smoke simulation in 3ds Max
  • Reusing velocity field data from another simulation tool
  • You need particle counts beyond what 3ds Max handles natively

Do not reach for it when

  • If you use Maya, Houdini or Blender, this does not apply. It is a 3ds Max plugin.
  • If you need full fluid or smoke simulation, use a dedicated solver. Stoke moves particles, it does not simulate the fluid.
  • Almost nobody outside 3ds Max visual effects work has a use for this.

One small example

An artist takes a FumeFX explosion and uses Stoke to send sparks tumbling along its motion, then renders them with Krakatoa.

How you pay

Free, licence included. Your only cost is the 3ds Max licence and the machines you simulate and render on.
