AWS Outposts rack

Compute  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Puts a full rack of AWS hardware inside your own building.

Picture it as

Renting a branch of the bank and having it built in your office lobby.

What it actually does

AWS ships a 42U rack, installs it in your data centre, and keeps running it. You use the same AWS console and APIs, but the machines sit on your floor. Only a subset of AWS services run on it, and you need real data centre space, power and cooling.

Reach for it when

  • Data must stay inside your building by law
  • An app needs very low delay to machines on site
  • You are moving off a data centre slowly and want one control plane

Do not reach for it when

  • If you just want cloud servers, use amazon-ec2 in a normal Region.
  • If you only need a small box at a shop or factory, use AWS Outposts servers instead.
  • If you only need a private network link back to AWS, use aws-direct-connect.
  • Most companies never need this. It is for large regulated or latency bound workloads.

One small example

A hospital runs its imaging app on an Outposts rack in its own basement so patient scans never leave the site.

How you pay

You pay per rack for a 3 year term, billed monthly, whether you use it or not. The surprise is that it is a long commitment, not pay as you go, and you still pay for data going back to the Region.
