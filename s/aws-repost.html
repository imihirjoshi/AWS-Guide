AWS rePost

Customer Enablement  ·  Learn this early

A free public question and answer site for AWS, run by AWS.

Picture it as

A public library help desk. Free, useful, but you wait for whoever is around to answer.

What it actually does

The replacement for the old AWS Forums. You ask a question in public and community members or AWS staff may answer. Answers are not guaranteed and not a support commitment. Quality varies, and for a specific account problem it is usually slower than a support ticket.

Reach for it when

  • A general how do I question about a service
  • You want to search what others already asked
  • You have no paid support plan

Do not reach for it when

  • Your production system is down, open an AWS Support case, do not post in public
  • The question involves account numbers, billing detail or anything private, it is a public site

One small example

You ask why a Lambda function times out reaching RDS, and somebody points you at the VPC subnet setup.
