AWS Distro for OpenTelemetry

Management and Governance  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

AWS's own build of the standard open source tool that collects traces and metrics from your app.

Picture it as

A generic medicine sold under a shop's own label. Same formula, packaged and supported by that shop.

What it actually does

OpenTelemetry is an industry standard for instrumenting code. ADOT is AWS's tested distribution of it, with the AWS exporters already included and AWS support behind it. It sends data to X-Ray, CloudWatch, Amazon Managed Prometheus, or to non AWS tools like Datadog. It is a packaging choice, not a new capability, and upstream OpenTelemetry works on AWS too.

Reach for it when

  • Instrumenting apps without locking into one vendor
  • Sending the same telemetry to AWS and a third party tool
  • Wanting AWS support on your collector build

Do not reach for it when

  • A pure AWS shop happy with the CloudWatch agent and X-Ray SDK, that is less work
  • Just wanting logs, CloudWatch Logs is far simpler

One small example

Your Kubernetes services run an ADOT collector that ships traces to X-Ray and metrics to Managed Prometheus.

How you pay

The distribution is free. You pay for what receives the data, and trace and custom metric ingestion in CloudWatch and X-Ray is the part that surprises people at volume.
