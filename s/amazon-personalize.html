Amazon Personalize

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Builds the "you might also like" list for your site from what your users actually did.

Picture it as

A shop assistant who remembers what you bought last time and points you at the aisle you probably want.

What it actually does

You send it user actions, item details and user details, it trains recommendation models, and you query it live for a ranked list for a given user. It handles new users and new items reasonably. It needs real scale to work, roughly a thousand users and tens of thousands of interactions minimum, and it is a black box, so you cannot easily explain any single recommendation.

Reach for it when

  • A catalogue with many items and steady traffic
  • Personalised ordering of a feed or search results
  • You have interaction history but no ML team

Do not reach for it when

  • A small catalogue, "most popular" and "bought together" queries in SQL get you most of the benefit
  • A new site with no user history, there is nothing to learn from
  • Recommendations you must justify to a user or regulator

One small example

A streaming site reorders the home page rows per viewer based on what they finished watching.

How you pay

Training hours, data ingest, and per thousand recommendation requests, plus an hourly charge for the minimum provisioned capacity on a live campaign. That idle hourly charge catches people.
