Amazon Interactive Video Service

Media  ·  Common, learn when you need it

It gives you live streaming with chat, like a Twitch style channel, with very little setup.

Picture it as

A walkie talkie set you take out of the box and use. No wiring, no engineer.

What it actually does

IVS takes an RTMP stream from software like OBS and delivers it to viewers in a few seconds, with a player, a chat service and viewer counts included. There is a real time mode for stages where several people appear on screen together with sub second delay. You give up control: fewer broadcast features than MediaLive, and the channel types and limits are fixed by AWS.

Reach for it when

  • Live shopping, creator streams, classes, gaming style channels
  • You want chat and low latency without building a video stack
  • Small teams with no broadcast experience

Do not reach for it when

  • Broadcast channels needing ad insertion and DRM, use MediaLive with MediaPackage and MediaTailor
  • Recorded video libraries, use MediaConvert with CloudFront
  • Very high hour counts, per hour delivery pricing gets expensive at scale

One small example

A jewellery seller runs a nightly live sale where viewers ask questions in chat and buy as they watch.

How you pay

Per hour of input and per hour of video delivered to viewers, plus chat messages. Delivery hours multiply by audience size, which is where the bill grows.
