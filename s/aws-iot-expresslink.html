AWS IoT ExpressLink

Internet of Things  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

A ready made connectivity chip, so a simple product can reach AWS without you writing network code.

Picture it as

A pre paid mobile dongle for a machine. You plug it in, send it plain text commands, and it handles the phone network and the security for you.

What it actually does

ExpressLink is not really a service you switch on, it is a specification that hardware makers such as Espressif and u blox build modules to. Your microcontroller talks to the module over a serial link with simple AT style commands, and the module handles Wi-Fi or cellular, TLS, certificates and the MQTT connection to IoT Core. It saves firmware effort at the cost of buying a specific module and accepting what it supports.

Reach for it when

  • A small product team with no embedded security experience
  • A microcontroller too small for a full TLS and MQTT stack
  • You want to get a connected product to market quickly

Do not reach for it when

  • A device already running Linux, just use the AWS IoT device SDK
  • Anything needing unusual protocols or fine control of the connection
  • Cases where the extra hardware cost per unit at scale outweighs the saved development time

One small example

A battery powered water leak alarm whose small chip sends one MQTT message by writing a single command line to the module.

How you pay

There is no AWS charge for ExpressLink itself. You pay for the module in your bill of materials and for the IoT Core messages it sends.
