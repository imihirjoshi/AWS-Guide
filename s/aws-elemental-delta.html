AWS Elemental Delta

Media  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It stores and hands out live video in your own building so thousands of viewers can watch it.

Picture it as

A newsagent who keeps the last hour of newspapers on the counter, so everyone arriving late can still take a copy.

What it actually does

Delta is on-premises software that packages a live feed into the formats players need, keeps a rolling window so viewers can pause or rewind, and serves it to a CDN. It is the older, self-hosted cousin of MediaPackage. It is legacy and rare; new work of this kind goes to MediaPackage in the cloud.

Reach for it when

  • An existing on-premises broadcast chain that already uses it
  • Time shift and catch up recording that must stay on site
  • Serving your own network of viewers without cloud delivery

Do not reach for it when

  • Anything new, use MediaPackage
  • Video on demand files, that is MediaConvert plus S3 and CloudFront
  • Small audiences, a plain origin server is cheaper

One small example

A cable operator lets subscribers rewind the last two hours of a channel from equipment in its own head end.

How you pay

Sold as software licences with appliances, not per stream. No hourly bill, but support and hardware are yours.
