AWS Security Incident Response

Security and Identity  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

AWS security experts help you handle a breach, and the service triages alerts in between.

Picture it as

A plumber on retainer. Day to day they check the meter, and when a pipe bursts at midnight you have a number that answers.

What it actually does

Launched at re:Invent 2024. It does two things: automatic triage of GuardDuty and Security Hub findings to cut noise, and, when something real happens, access to the AWS Customer Incident Response Team with a case workflow and communications. It is help with an AWS-side incident. It is not a full managed security operations centre, and it does not cover your code, your laptops or your SaaS.

Reach for it when

  • You have no security team of your own and AWS is where your risk lives
  • You want a defined path to AWS experts before an incident, not during
  • GuardDuty noise nobody triages

Do not reach for it when

  • Not a replacement for detection, you still need GuardDuty switched on
  • If you already have a mature SOC or an MDR vendor, this overlaps
  • Not for non-AWS incidents like a compromised laptop

One small example

GuardDuty flags credentials used from an unusual country, and the service escalates a real case to AWS responders instead of it sitting unread.

How you pay

Originally a large monthly subscription with a high minimum. It now bills per finding ingested, with the first 10,000 a month free, and it is included with Enterprise Support.
