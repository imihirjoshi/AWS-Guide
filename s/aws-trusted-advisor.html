AWS Trusted Advisor

Management and Governance  ·  Common, learn when you need it

Checks your account and suggests fixes.

Picture it as

A yearly service for your car. It lists what needs attention.

What it actually does

Trusted Advisor checks for wasted money, security holes, missing redundancy and hitting limits. A few checks are free on every account, the full set needs a paid support plan.

Reach for it when

  • Regular account health checks

One small example

Flagging an S3 bucket that is open to the public.

How you pay

Some checks free. Full set needs Business support or above.

AWS Cost ExplorerShows where your AWS money is going.AWS ConfigRecords how your AWS setup changes over time.AWS Security HubOne screen that gathers security problems from all your AWS security tools.
