Amazon Nova

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Common, learn when you need it

Amazon's own family of AI models, sold through Bedrock.

Picture it as

A supermarket's own-brand range sitting on the shelf next to the big names, cheaper, and fine for a lot of jobs.

What it actually does

A set of models rather than a service: Micro, Lite, Pro and Premier for text and reasoning, Canvas for images, Reel for video, and Sonic for speech in and speech out. They are notably cheap per token and fast. On hard reasoning and coding they generally trail the frontier models from Anthropic and others, so people often use Nova for high volume simple work and something stronger for the hard calls.

Reach for it when

  • High volume classification, extraction or summarising where cost matters
  • You want image or video generation inside Bedrock
  • Low latency speech conversations with Nova Sonic

Do not reach for it when

  • Difficult reasoning, long code changes or agent work, pick a stronger model on Bedrock
  • Running outside AWS, Nova is Bedrock only

One small example

Tagging a million support emails by topic with Nova Micro for a few dollars.

How you pay

Per million input and output tokens, at the cheap end of Bedrock. The surprise is not the token price but provisioned throughput, which bills by the hour whether you use it or not.
