Amazon WorkMail

Business Applications  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

AWS business email and calendar. It is being shut down on 31 March 2027.

Picture it as

A company post room that is closing. It still sorts your post today, but you have been told the date it stops.

What it actually does

Amazon WorkMail is a managed business email and calendar service. It gives your staff mailboxes on your own domain, with shared calendars and contacts. People can use Microsoft Outlook on Windows or Mac, phone mail apps, IMAP clients, or a web browser. It can link to Active Directory and it encrypts mail using keys you control in AWS KMS. The important fact comes first though. AWS has said it will stop supporting Amazon WorkMail on 31 March 2027. After that date you will not be able to get into it. AWS has published migration guidance. So this is not a product to start a new business on.

Reach for it when

  • Almost nobody should start with this now, because of the 2027 end date
  • Existing WorkMail customers, who should be planning a move off it
  • Reading up on it only to understand a system you have inherited

Do not reach for it when

  • Do not pick this for new email. It ends on 31 March 2027. Use Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace instead.
  • Do not use it to send bulk or app email. That is Amazon Simple Email Service, which is a different thing and is not going away.
  • Do not confuse it with Amazon Simple Email Service. WorkMail is staff mailboxes. SES is mail your software sends.
  • If you are on it today, start the migration now rather than near the date.

One small example

A 30 person firm gave every person a mailbox on their own domain and used Outlook as usual. That firm now needs to move to another mail provider before March 2027.

How you pay

4 US dollars per user per month, including 50 GB of storage per user. That price is still listed, but the service ends in March 2027, so the real cost to you includes a migration.
