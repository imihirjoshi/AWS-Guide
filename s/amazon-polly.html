Amazon Polly

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Common, learn when you need it

Turns written text into a natural sounding voice.

Picture it as

A newsreader who will read anything you hand over.

What it actually does

Polly converts text to speech in many languages and voices, including Indian English and Hindi. Neural voices sound noticeably better than the standard ones.

Reach for it when

  • Audio versions of lessons
  • Accessibility
  • Voice notifications

Do not reach for it when

  • You need a specific human's voice.

One small example

Turning a written lesson into an audio file students can listen to.

How you pay

Per million characters.

Usually sits next to

Amazon TranscribeTurns speech into written text.Amazon TranslateTranslates text between languages.
