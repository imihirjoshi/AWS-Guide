Amazon WorkDocs

Business Applications  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Amazon's file storage and sharing service for staff, now switched off.

Picture it as

An office filing cabinet that the company has already emptied and taken away.

What it actually does

It was a Dropbox or Google Drive style service with sync clients, sharing links and commenting on documents. AWS stopped new sign-ups in April 2024 and ended support on 25 April 2025, after which data was purged. The site, the APIs and WorkDocs Drive are all gone. Listed here only so you recognise the name in old documentation.

Reach for it when

  • Nothing, it is closed

Do not reach for it when

  • Team file sharing, use Google Drive, SharePoint, OneDrive or Dropbox
  • File storage for an application, use Amazon S3, or Amazon FSx or EFS for a network drive

One small example

An admin ran the bulk export in early 2025 and moved the company's shared folders to SharePoint before the purge.

How you pay

Nothing, it is closed.
