AWS Audit Manager

Security and Identity  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It collects evidence about your AWS setup automatically so an audit is less painful.

Picture it as

A clerk who quietly photocopies every receipt all year, so at tax time the shoebox is already sorted into folders.

What it actually does

Audit Manager runs against a framework, like SOC 2, PCI DSS or HIPAA, and keeps pulling evidence from CloudTrail, Config and Security Hub into an assessment you can export. It gathers and organises evidence. It does not decide you passed, and it does not fix anything. A human auditor still reads the report, and plenty of controls still need manual evidence you upload yourself.

Reach for it when

  • You are going through a real, repeated compliance audit
  • You need a paper trail of control evidence over time
  • Many accounts and you cannot collect evidence by hand

Do not reach for it when

  • Not for finding or fixing security problems, use Security Hub CSPM and Inspector
  • Overkill if you have no actual audit coming, it costs money and attention for nothing
  • Not proof of AWS's own compliance, that is Artifact

One small example

A fintech enables the PCI DSS framework, and a year of evidence for each control is ready when the assessor arrives.

How you pay

Charged per resource assessment, so cost rises with how many resources each control touches. Enabling many frameworks at once across a big account is the surprise.
