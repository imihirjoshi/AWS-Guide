AWS Firewall Manager

Security and Identity  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It pushes the same firewall rules to every account in your company automatically.

Picture it as

A head office rule that every branch must lock the back door, applied centrally so a new branch is locked on day one.

What it actually does

Firewall Manager sits above WAF, Shield Advanced, Network Firewall, Route 53 Resolver DNS Firewall and security groups, and applies your policies across all accounts in an AWS Organization, including accounts created later. It is a policy distributor, not a firewall itself. It needs AWS Organizations, a nominated admin account and, for some policies, Shield Advanced, so it is useless to a single-account setup.

Reach for it when

  • Many AWS accounts under an Organization
  • You must guarantee a new account cannot ship without WAF
  • Auditing for stray, overly open security groups across accounts

Do not reach for it when

  • Pointless with one or two accounts, just configure WAF or Network Firewall directly
  • It does not inspect traffic itself, the underlying services do that and bill separately

One small example

Security mandates that every public ALB in the company has the managed WAF rule set, and Firewall Manager applies it to accounts opened next month too.

How you pay

A monthly fee per policy, per region, on top of the full cost of every WAF, Network Firewall or Shield resource it creates. The underlying services are the big number.
