AWS IoT FleetWise

Internet of Things  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It collects data from vehicles, choosing what to send so you do not pay for every signal.

Picture it as

A dashcam that only saves the clip when something interesting happens, rather than filming the whole journey and posting you the tapes.

What it actually does

You describe a vehicle's signals from its CAN bus or other network, then write campaigns that say which signals to collect and under what conditions, for example brake pressure only when speed is over eighty. An agent in the vehicle applies that and uploads to AWS. It is a narrow service for carmakers and fleet operators, and it needs access to the vehicle's signal database, so most people cannot use it at all.

Reach for it when

  • You build or operate vehicles and control their software
  • Mobile data costs make sending everything impossible
  • Engineering teams investigating faults across a model range

Do not reach for it when

  • Tracking where vehicles are, a GPS tracker into IoT Core is far cheaper
  • Non vehicle machinery, use IoT SiteWise
  • Anyone without access to the vehicle bus and signal definitions

One small example

Collecting battery cell temperatures from ten thousand electric cars, but only during fast charging.

How you pay

Per vehicle per month plus per signal message collected. Campaigns that trigger more often than expected drive the cost up quickly.
