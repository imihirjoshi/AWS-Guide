Amazon VPC Lattice

Networking and Content Delivery  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Connects your services to each other by name, with permissions and routing handled for you.

Picture it as

An office receptionist who knows every department, checks whether you are allowed to speak to them, and puts the call through. You never need their extension number.

What it actually does

You register services into a service network and they call each other by a stable name, across VPCs and AWS accounts, without peering or Transit Gateway. It applies IAM permissions per request and handles retries and traffic splitting. It is AWS's recommended replacement for App Mesh, and it is still fairly new with a smaller community than Kubernetes service meshes.

Reach for it when

  • Services spread across several VPCs or accounts that need to call each other
  • You want per request authorisation between services, not just network rules
  • Moving off AWS App Mesh before it shuts down

Do not reach for it when

  • Everything in one VPC, a load balancer and security groups are simpler and cheaper
  • You need deep mesh features and portability, use Istio on EKS

One small example

A checkout service in one account calls an inventory service in another, by name, with an IAM policy allowing only that caller.

How you pay

An hourly fee per service and per service network, plus per GB and per request. The standing hourly fees mean a network of many small services costs money before it serves a single request.
