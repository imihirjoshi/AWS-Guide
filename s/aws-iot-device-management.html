AWS IoT Device Management

Internet of Things  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It helps you keep track of and update a large number of devices at once.

Picture it as

A fleet manager for a delivery company. It keeps the list of every van, groups them by depot, and rolls out a change to one group at a time instead of all at once.

What it actually does

It sits on top of IoT Core and adds bulk registration, searchable device groups, over the air software update jobs, and secure tunnelling so you can open a remote shell to a device behind a firewall. Jobs can be rolled out gradually and stopped automatically if too many devices fail. It manages devices, it does not run your device software.

Reach for it when

  • Thousands of devices in the field needing firmware updates
  • You need to find devices by attribute, like model or region
  • You need remote access to a device for support

Do not reach for it when

  • Under a hundred devices, the plain IoT Core registry and your own scripts are enough
  • Servers or containers, use Systems Manager instead
  • Devices you can physically reach to update

One small example

Pushing a firmware update to five thousand vending machines, ten percent at a time, pausing if failures pass five percent.

How you pay

Per device registered, per bulk registration, per search query, and per minute of secure tunnelling. Tunnelling sessions left open are a common unwanted charge.
