AWS IoT Device Defender

Internet of Things  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It watches your connected devices for signs that one has been tampered with.

Picture it as

A building security guard who knows the normal routine. If a door that opens twice a day suddenly opens forty times at three in the morning, the guard raises it.

What it actually does

It has two parts. Audit checks your AWS setup for weak settings, like certificates that are shared between devices, expiring, or policies that are too open. Detect watches device behaviour against rules or a learned baseline and alerts on odd traffic. Note that Device Defender Detect moves to maintenance and access is restricted from 31 August 2026, so do not build new work on Detect; Audit is unaffected.

Reach for it when

  • A fleet where a stolen device could be used to attack you
  • You need evidence of security checks for an auditor
  • Certificate and policy hygiene across many devices

Do not reach for it when

  • Small fleets, where reading CloudWatch metrics yourself is enough
  • Account wide threat detection, that is GuardDuty
  • New behaviour monitoring work, since Detect is being retired, build alerting on CloudWatch and your own rules

One small example

An alert when one smart lock starts opening two hundred MQTT connections an hour instead of its usual one.

How you pay

Per device per month for audit checks, and per behaviour metric checked for Detect. Costs grow with the number of behaviours you define, not just device count.
