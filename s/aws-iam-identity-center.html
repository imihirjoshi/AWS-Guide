AWS IAM Identity Center

Security and Identity  ·  Learn this early

One login that gets your staff into all your AWS accounts, with the right permissions.

Picture it as

A single office badge that opens the doors you are allowed through, instead of a separate key for every room.

What it actually does

Formerly AWS Single Sign-On, and still called that in older docs. It gives each person one sign-in, often from Okta, Entra ID or Google Workspace, and hands out short-lived credentials for each AWS account and role. This is the current recommended way for humans to access AWS. Long-lived IAM users with access keys are the old way and are discouraged.

Reach for it when

  • More than one AWS account, or more than about two people
  • You want to stop creating IAM users and access keys
  • You already have a company identity provider to plug in

Do not reach for it when

  • Not for applications or servers, they should use IAM roles
  • Not for your product's end users, use Cognito
  • Not a replacement for IAM policies, it hands them out, it does not define what they mean

One small example

A new engineer is added to one group in Okta and instantly has read-only access to production and admin in the sandbox account.

How you pay

Free. You pay for nothing here, which is why there is no excuse not to use it.
