AWS Elemental Server

Media  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Software in your own building that converts recorded video files into streaming formats.

Picture it as

Your own photocopier in the office instead of walking to the copy shop.

What it actually does

Elemental Server is the on-premises file transcoder, the local version of what MediaConvert does in the cloud. It runs on appliances or your own servers and is used where the source files must not leave the building, or where a facility already owns the hardware. It is legacy broadcast kit, and very few new buyers pick it over MediaConvert.

Reach for it when

  • Media archives that cannot legally leave the premises
  • A facility with spare hardware and steady, predictable volume
  • Existing Elemental installations being extended

Do not reach for it when

  • Cloud workflows, MediaConvert needs no hardware
  • Bursty volumes, you pay for capacity you are not using
  • Small teams, there is nobody to maintain it

One small example

A broadcaster converts its master tapes to streaming files on machines inside its own archive facility.

How you pay

Licence and appliance purchase through AWS sales, plus support. Your own power, space and staff are the real running cost.
