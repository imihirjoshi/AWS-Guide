AWS App Studio

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Describe a business app in plain words and it builds a working one for you.

Picture it as

Telling a joiner "I need a cupboard with three shelves and a lock" and getting the cupboard, rather than the wood.

What it actually does

You type what the app should do, and it generates the data model, the screens and the logic, then hosts it for you. It is aimed at internal business apps, approvals, trackers, small workflows, built by people who are not developers. It is narrow: you cannot export the code to run elsewhere, styling is limited, and anything unusual needs a developer anyway. It sits opposite tools like Power Apps and Retool.

Reach for it when

  • A small internal tool nobody has time to build properly
  • A non-developer team needs a form and a workflow on AWS data
  • Prototyping a process before committing engineering time

Do not reach for it when

  • Anything customer-facing or with real design needs, build a normal web app
  • Apps you may need to move off AWS later, there is no code to take with you
  • Heavy logic or integrations, a developer with Lambda and a frontend will be faster

One small example

An operations team builds a leave request tracker that reads staff records and emails the manager.

How you pay

Per hour that someone is actively building, and per hour that published apps are used. Publishing an app many staff use all day is where the cost lands.
