AWS Supply Chain

Business Applications  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It reads your existing ERP data and warns you when stock is about to run short.

Picture it as

A shop manager who walks the stockroom every morning, checks what is arriving, and tells you which shelf will be empty on Thursday.

What it actually does

It connects to SAP, Oracle or flat files, builds a data lake of your inventory, orders and suppliers, and then runs demand forecasting, supply planning and risk alerts on top. It is a finished application, not a toolkit. Honestly, it is a niche product with few users, and its value depends almost entirely on how clean your ERP data is. Getting the data mapped in is most of the work.

Reach for it when

  • You already run SAP or Oracle and want forecasting without a new full system
  • You want one view of inventory across warehouses
  • You need supplier lead time risk alerts

Do not reach for it when

  • You are small or your data lives in spreadsheets, a normal inventory tool or even Excel is cheaper and faster
  • You want to build your own supply chain logic, use Glue, Athena and SageMaker instead of a closed app

One small example

A mid sized electronics maker sees an alert that a component's lead time doubled and a build slot in six weeks is now at risk.

How you pay

Priced per module per unit hour, so you pay for demand planning, supply planning and insights separately, plus data lake storage. The surprise is that turning on several modules multiplies the bill.
