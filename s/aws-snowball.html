Basics / Services / Storage

AWS Snowball

Storage  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

AWS posts you a rugged box, you copy data onto it and post it back.

Picture it as

Posting a hard drive instead of emailing a huge file, because the post office is faster than your broadband.

What it actually does

Snowball Edge devices hold tens of terabytes and also run EC2 and Lambda locally, which made them useful at ships, mines and army bases. The family is closing down. Snowmobile, the truck, is gone, Snowcone was discontinued in 2024, and as of late 2025 Snowball Edge is open only to existing customers, with support ending in commercial regions on 31 December 2026. Treat it as retired and do not plan new work on it.

Reach for it when

  • Almost never now, the service is being withdrawn
  • Only if you already have devices under an existing arrangement

Do not reach for it when

  • For bulk transfer over a network, use AWS DataSync
  • For a permanent fast private link, use AWS Direct Connect
  • For edge compute in a remote site, use AWS Outposts servers or ordinary hardware

One small example

A research ship used to fill a Snowball Edge with sonar data at sea and ship it home at port. Today that team would plan for DataSync over satellite or a Direct Connect at dock.

How you pay

Historically a per job fee plus per day device rental, with shipping. Moot now, since new orders are closed.

Usually sits next to

Amazon Simple Storage ServiceA place to keep files, as many as you want.AWS DataSyncIt copies large amounts of files from one place to another, and checks they arrived.
