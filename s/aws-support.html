AWS Support

Customer Enablement  ·  Learn this early

A paid plan that lets you raise a ticket and get a human at AWS to help.

Picture it as

An extended warranty with a phone number, where the price decides how fast somebody picks up.

What it actually does

Four paid tiers above the free Basic account help. Developer is cheap and business hours only. Business gives 24/7 technical help and one hour response for a production outage. Enterprise On-Ramp and Enterprise add faster response and a named technical account manager. Support covers AWS services, not bugs in your own code.

Reach for it when

  • You run anything in production
  • You need somebody to call during an outage
  • You want a service limit raised quickly

Do not reach for it when

  • You just want a general question answered, re:Post is free
  • You want somebody to write or debug your application, support will not do that, hire a Partner or Professional Services

One small example

Your RDS instance fails over at midnight and you open a severity one case that gets a reply in under an hour.

How you pay

Developer starts around $29 a month, Business around $100 a month, but both are really a percentage of your AWS spend once it grows, which is the bit that surprises people. Enterprise On-Ramp starts around $5,500 a month and Enterprise around $15,000 a month.
