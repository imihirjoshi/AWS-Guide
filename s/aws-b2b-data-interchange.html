AWS B2B Data Interchange

Application Integration  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

It turns the old-fashioned trading files big companies exchange into JSON your software can read, and back again.

Picture it as

A translator between two companies who insist on writing to each other in a 1980s telex format.

What it actually does

It handles EDI, the standard format retailers, logistics firms and hospitals use for purchase orders and invoices. You define trading partners and mapping templates, and it converts X12 EDI into JSON or XML on the way in, and back to EDI on the way out. It supports X12, so EDIFACT and HL7 users are mostly out of luck. Only relevant if a partner forces EDI on you.

Reach for it when

  • A large retailer will only send you orders as X12 EDI
  • Replacing an ageing in-house EDI translator
  • You already handle files in S3 and want the parsing managed

Do not reach for it when

  • Nobody is asking you for EDI, then you do not need it, use a normal API
  • You need EDIFACT or HL7 or full partner communications with AS2, a specialist EDI vendor covers more

One small example

A supplier receives an 850 purchase order from a supermarket chain and it arrives in their order system as plain JSON.

How you pay

Per transaction processed plus a small charge per active trading partner. Low volumes are cheap, so the real cost is the mapping setup work.
