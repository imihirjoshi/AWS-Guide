Basics / Services / Compute

AWS Outposts family

Compute  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

AWS ships actual racks or small boxes of its own hardware to your building and runs them for you.

Picture it as

A coffee chain installing one of its own branded machines in your office lobby, still serviced by their staff, still on their system.

What it actually does

Outposts come as full 42U racks or as 1U and 2U servers. They run a subset of AWS services on your premises, managed by AWS and connected back to a Region. You need real datacentre conditions, power, cooling and a solid network link, and if that link drops your control plane goes with it. The service list is much shorter than a Region's.

Reach for it when

  • Data legally required to stay in a specific building or country
  • Factory or hospital systems that need very low latency to local machines
  • Migrating slowly while old hardware still sits on site

Do not reach for it when

  • Just wanting things nearer users, use Local Zones or CloudFront
  • Short projects, these are multi-year commitments
  • Small offices without proper power, cooling and network

One small example

A hospital runs its imaging application on an Outposts rack in its own basement so scans never leave the building.

How you pay

You buy or commit for one or three years, upfront or monthly, per rack. The surprise is that the commitment runs whether you use the capacity or not, plus the cost of the network link home.
