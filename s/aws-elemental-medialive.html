AWS Elemental MediaLive

Media  ·  Common, learn when you need it

It takes a live camera or stream and turns it into something viewers can watch.

Picture it as

The control room at a TV station. The camera feed comes in, and it goes out in the shapes every screen in the country can show.

What it actually does

MediaLive is the live encoder. You point a feed at it, it produces several quality levels, inserts ad markers, adds captions and overlays, and passes the result to MediaPackage or your own destination. Channels are billed while they are running whether or not anyone is watching, and stopping a channel is a manual step people forget.

Reach for it when

  • Live events, sport, worship services, conferences
  • 24/7 channels that need broadcast features like ad insertion
  • Live feeds that must go out in several bitrates

Do not reach for it when

  • Simple low latency streaming with chat, Amazon IVS is far less work
  • Recorded files, use MediaConvert
  • Occasional casual streams, a third party service is cheaper than running a channel

One small example

A football club streams home matches, with MediaLive producing 1080p, 720p and 480p versions live.

How you pay

Per hour of channel running time, by input and output class, plus data out. The surprise is being charged for a channel left running after the event ended.
