AWS User Notifications

Management and Governance  ·  Common, learn when you need it

One place to decide which AWS events reach you, and where they land.

Picture it as

The notification settings screen on your phone, for the whole of AWS.

What it actually does

You set rules like send me EC2 state changes and CloudWatch alarms in these regions, and choose email, chat, or push to the mobile app. It gives a single inbox in the console instead of scattered per service settings. It is a router over EventBridge events, so if an event does not exist in EventBridge, it cannot notify you. It is fairly new and still thin on options.

Reach for it when

  • Wanting one consistent place for AWS alerts
  • Cross region and cross account alerting without wiring each service
  • Push notifications to the mobile console app

Do not reach for it when

  • Notifying your own end users, use SNS or Pinpoint
  • Complex routing, filtering and retries, build on EventBridge and SNS directly

One small example

You get one email whenever any account in the organisation has a security finding, instead of per account rules.

How you pay

The service is free. You pay for the underlying delivery, and a noisy rule sending high volume email is what annoys people.
