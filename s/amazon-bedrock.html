Amazon Bedrock

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Learn this early

Use ready made AI models, including Claude, through one API.

Picture it as

A tap. The water treatment plant already exists, you just open the tap.

What it actually does

Bedrock gives access to foundation models from several providers without managing any servers. Your data is not used to train the models. It supports retrieval from your own documents and agents that take actions.

Reach for it when

  • Adding AI to an app
  • Summarising, extracting, chatting, classifying

Do not reach for it when

  • A simple rule would do the job.
  • You need to train on your own data from scratch. Use SageMaker.

One small example

Reading a scanned invoice and pulling out the amount and date.

How you pay

Per thousand input and output tokens. Provisioned capacity is also available.

Usually sits next to

Amazon SageMaker AIBuild and train your own machine learning models.Amazon TextractPulls text and tables out of scanned documents.AWS LambdaRun a small piece of code without owning a server.
