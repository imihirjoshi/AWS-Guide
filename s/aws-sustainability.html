AWS Sustainability

Management and Governance  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Not a service. It is AWS's environment programme, with one real tool for your carbon numbers.

Picture it as

A company's recycling policy, plus a single electricity meter you can actually read.

What it actually does

There is no service you can switch on called AWS Sustainability. The name covers AWS's own renewable energy commitments, the Sustainability pillar of the Well-Architected Framework, which is guidance, and one concrete tool, the Customer Carbon Footprint Tool in the Billing console. That tool shows estimated emissions from your AWS usage, by month, region and service.

Reach for it when

  • Your company has to report cloud emissions for an ESG disclosure
  • You want to compare the carbon cost of regions before choosing one
  • Reviewing a design against the Well-Architected sustainability guidance

Do not reach for it when

  • If you want live, detailed, per-resource carbon data, this is too coarse. It is monthly and estimated.
  • If your real goal is to spend less money, use AWS Cost Explorer. Less waste usually means less carbon anyway.
  • Do not expect an API to call. Most of it is documents and a billing dashboard.

One small example

Your finance team downloads last quarter's carbon report from the Billing console for the annual ESG statement.

How you pay

Free. The surprise is the delay and the coarseness. Data appears about three weeks after month end, and anything under half a kilo of CO2e shows as zero.
