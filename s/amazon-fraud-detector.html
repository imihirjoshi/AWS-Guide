Amazon Fraud Detector

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Scores an online action as likely fraud or not, using your own past cases.

Picture it as

A shop manager who has seen enough shoplifters to get a feeling about a particular customer at the till.

What it actually does

You upload historic events labelled fraud or legitimate, it trains a model, and you then send live events and get a score from 0 to 1000 plus an outcome from rules you write. It needs a decent volume of labelled history, roughly ten thousand events with at least a few hundred frauds, so a new business usually cannot use it. It scores, it does not block, your code has to act on the outcome.

Reach for it when

  • Account signup abuse or fake reviews at reasonable volume
  • Guest checkout payment fraud where you have labelled history
  • You want rules and a model in one place

Do not reach for it when

  • Card payment fraud where your payment provider already screens, you would pay twice for the same check
  • A new product with no fraud history, start with hand-written rules
  • Anything where you must explain each decision precisely to a regulator

One small example

A signup from a fresh email on a known hosting IP scores 890, so your code sends it to manual review.

How you pay

Per prediction, plus training and hosting hours. Scoring every page view rather than the few risky moments is the classic overspend.
