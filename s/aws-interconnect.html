AWS Interconnect

Networking and Content Delivery  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

Orders a private cable between AWS and another cloud or your office, in a few clicks.

Picture it as

Asking the phone company for a dedicated line instead of running the wire yourself.

What it actually does

It is a newer managed connectivity service that arranges private, fixed bandwidth links from AWS to Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud, or to your own sites through a network partner. The point is that AWS handles the paperwork and router setup that used to take weeks. It is aimed at large multicloud setups, so most teams will never open it.

Reach for it when

  • You run workloads in AWS and another cloud and want them talking privately
  • Setting up a cross connect by hand with a carrier is taking weeks
  • You need encrypted, predictable bandwidth between providers

Do not reach for it when

  • You only need AWS to your own data centre. Plain aws-direct-connect is the well worn path.
  • Your traffic is small or occasional. A aws-site-to-site-vpn over the internet costs far less.
  • You only use one cloud. Then there is nothing here for you.

One small example

A bank links its AWS region to its Azure tenancy with a 10 Gbps private path instead of routing over the internet.

How you pay

By the link, on capacity and how long you keep it. Fixed bandwidth means you pay whether or not traffic flows.
