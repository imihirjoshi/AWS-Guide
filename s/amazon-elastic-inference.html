Amazon Elastic Inference

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

A retired way to bolt a slice of a GPU onto a normal server.

Picture it as

Renting a quarter of a power tool instead of the whole thing. The rental shop has since closed.

What it actually does

It let you attach a fraction of GPU acceleration to an EC2 instance or SageMaker endpoint, so you paid less than a full GPU for models that only needed a little help. AWS stopped taking new customers in April 2023 and has wound it down. Do not design anything around it. The modern answers are Inferentia instances, SageMaker serverless inference, or just a small GPU instance.

Reach for it when

  • Nothing. It is retired

Do not reach for it when

  • Cheap inference today, use Inferentia (inf1/inf2) or SageMaker serverless inference
  • Occasional inference, use Lambda or a Bedrock model and skip servers entirely

One small example

An exam question from 2021 offers it as the cheap way to run a PyTorch model, which is no longer the real answer.

How you pay

It billed per accelerator-hour alongside the instance. Not relevant now.
