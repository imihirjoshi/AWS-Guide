AWS Panorama

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Advanced, most people never need it

A discontinued box that ran computer vision on existing security cameras.

Picture it as

A clever set top box wired into the CCTV, watching the feeds so a person did not have to. The maker has now stopped supporting it.

What it actually does

Panorama was a hardware appliance plus a service that took feeds from ordinary IP cameras and ran vision models on site, so video never left the building. AWS stopped taking new customers in May 2025 and ends support on 31 May 2026, after which the console and resources go away. Do not start anything new on it. AWS points people to SageMaker for the models and IoT Greengrass, EKS or ECS Anywhere for running them on site.

Reach for it when

  • Only relevant if you already own appliances and are planning your exit before May 2026

Do not reach for it when

  • For any new on-site vision project, build the model in SageMaker AI and deploy it with AWS IoT Greengrass instead
  • If the video can go to the cloud, plain cloud inference on EC2 or SageMaker is simpler than edge hardware

One small example

A factory used Panorama to spot missing safety helmets on its existing camera feeds, and is now moving that model to Greengrass on its own hardware.

How you pay

Moot now. It was appliance hardware bought up front plus a monthly charge per deployed model per device.
