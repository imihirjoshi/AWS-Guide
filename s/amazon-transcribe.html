Amazon Transcribe

AI and Machine Learning  ·  Common, learn when you need it

Turns speech into written text.

Picture it as

A stenographer who types everything that is said.

What it actually does

Transcribe converts audio and video to text, with timestamps and speaker labels. It handles Indian English. Accuracy drops with heavy background noise or heavy code switching.

Reach for it when

  • Subtitles for lessons
  • Meeting notes
  • Making audio searchable

Do not reach for it when

  • Very poor audio. Fix the recording first.

One small example

Auto subtitles on a recorded class.

How you pay

Per second of audio.

Usually sits next to

Amazon PollyTurns written text into a natural sounding voice.Amazon TranslateTranslates text between languages.
