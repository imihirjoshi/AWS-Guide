AWS Management Console

Management and Governance  ·  Learn this early

The AWS website where you click around to see and change your stuff.

Picture it as

The dashboard of a car. Everything is reachable, but you still need to know what each dial does.

What it actually does

It is the browser interface covering every AWS service, plus search, billing and a built in shell called CloudShell. It is the right place to learn and to look at things. It is the wrong place to build production infrastructure, because clicks are not recorded, not repeatable, and nobody can review them. Experienced teams read here and write with code.

Reach for it when

  • Learning what a service does
  • Checking a resource or a bill quickly
  • One off emergency action during an incident

Do not reach for it when

  • Creating production infrastructure, use CloudFormation, CDK or Terraform
  • Anything repeated more than twice, script it with the AWS CLI or an SDK

One small example

You open the S3 page to check why a bucket is bigger than expected, then fix the lifecycle rule in Terraform.

How you pay

Free to use. Everything you click into costs whatever that service costs, and forgotten resources created here are a classic surprise bill.
