Accuracy

The explanations are deliberately simplified so a beginner can follow them. Simplification loses detail, and AWS changes services constantly, so parts of this site will be out of date at any given moment.

Do not make a technical, financial or architectural decision based on this site alone. Check the official AWS documentation.

Cost figures

Every number in the playground is a rough teaching estimate. It assumes approximate US East on demand list pricing and ignores the free tier, data transfer, regional differences, taxes, Reserved Instances, Savings Plans, Spot pricing, enterprise discounts and support charges.

Real bills differ, sometimes by a lot. These figures are not a quote, not a budget and not financial advice. Use the AWS Pricing Calculator for anything that matters.

The playground creates nothing

The sandbox is a drawing tool. It has no AWS credentials, makes no AWS API calls, and cannot create, change or delete anything in any AWS account. Nothing you draw here exists anywhere.
